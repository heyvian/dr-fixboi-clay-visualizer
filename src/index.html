      How to use the clay lab

      Load a print. Drop in a G-code file up to 25 MB or explore the sample vase.

      Check placement. A red ring and arrow show the direction and distance to X0 Y0 when the print’s center is over 50 mm away. A solid red disk marks a print whose lowest deposition is above Z0, with a label showing the gap. Travel moves are ignored. The red labels lie flat on the bed beside the markings and turn as you orbit. Render tints the canvas weave with the markings and lettering. Check G-code also flags issues such as out-of-order layers and deposition below Z0.

      Set up the print. The default nozzle is 4 mm. Lower viscosity makes the bead wider and flatter. Turn on Simulate gravity to estimate sag and compression. Fast gives a quick preview. Accurate uses finer deposition steps and estimates wet clay flow. Experimental VIC highlights failure hotspots and demonstrates deposition, tearing, and collapse.

      Inspect the shape. Drag to orbit, or use the arrow keys with the preview focused. Scroll or use the camera buttons to zoom. Turn on Dimensions to show length, width, and height.

      Preview firing. Pick a clay body and switch between Greenware and Fired. Shrinkage overlay ghosts the as-printed vessel and shows both sets of dimensions.

      Render the finish. Turn on Render to see the clay and canvas under studio lights. Choose Low or High for render quality. High adds softer shadows, accent lighting, and finer surface detail.

      Your G-code stays on this device, and your browser remembers your settings.
